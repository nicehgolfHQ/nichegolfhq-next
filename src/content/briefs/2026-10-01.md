- [AJGA Junior All-Star Invitational Returns to Le Triomphe Oct. 8-11 With 72 of the World's Best 12-to-15-Year-Olds](https://www.ajga.org/tournaments/2026145) — AJGA
  The 54-hole stroke-play invitational heads back to Le Triomphe Golf and Country Club in Broussard, La., for its 27th year at the venue. The 72-player field is built from the 2026 AJGA Junior All-Star Team, champions of this year's Junior All-Star Series events, and the Rolex AJGA Rankings.

- [Nate Smith and Andrew Bailey Meet in 36-Hole U.S. Mid-Amateur Final Today at The Lido](https://championships.usga.org/usmidamateur/2026/scoring.html) — USGA
  Nate Smith of Tetonia, Idaho, beat Mikel Martinson 2 and 1 to reach his first USGA final, while Andrew Bailey of Cleveland, the two-time Ohio Amateur champion, took out Matt Vogt 4 and 2. The 36-hole final tees off Thursday morning on The Lido, with a 2027 U.S. Open exemption at Pebble Beach and a likely Masters invitation awaiting the winner.
  Tags: mid-am

- [Frazier and Hanzel Capture Dale Morey Titles as Society of Seniors Wraps 44th Championship at The Landings](https://www.societyofseniors.com/news/frazier-hanzel-winners-at-the-landings/) — Society of Seniors
  Jeff Frazier of Mechanicsburg, Pa., closed out a four-shot lead to win the Senior division at 6-under 210, capping a year in which he swept all four GAP Senior Majors. In the Super Senior division, 2013 U.S. Senior Amateur champion Doug Hanzel won by six at 11-under 205 on his home Deer Creek course, becoming a four-time Dale Morey champion.
  Tags: senior
