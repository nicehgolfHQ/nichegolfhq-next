- [Behr Wins Three-Way Playoff for Georgia Mid-Amateur Title at Lookout Mountain](https://www.gsga.org/competitions/results/georgia-mid-amateur-championship) — GSGA
  Stephen Behr started the final round four shots back and shot a 4-under 66 at Lookout Mountain Golf Club to force a playoff, then beat Blake Maum and defending champion Hayes Brown on the second playoff hole. Weather cut the 45th Georgia Mid-Amateur to 36 holes.

  The 54-hole, WAGR-counting event runs Oct. 16-18 at Soule Park in Ojai, Calif. It has Senior (55+) and Super Senior (65+) divisions, and two-time champion Tim Hogarth leads the history book.
