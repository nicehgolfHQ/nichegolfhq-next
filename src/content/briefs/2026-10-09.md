- [AJGA PING Invitational at Karsten Creek Cancelled Due to Weather](https://www.ajga.org/tournaments/2026144) — AJGA
  The AJGA cancelled the boys-only PING Invitational (Oct. 9-12, Stillwater, Okla.), one of its top-tier invitationals. Players in the field who played five other ranked events this season still qualify for year-end Rolex Junior All-America honors.
  Tags: juniors

- [45th Georgia Mid-Amateur Tees Off Today at Lookout Mountain Golf Club](https://www.gsga.org/competitions/results/georgia-mid-amateur-championship) — GSGA
  A field of 144 players starts 54 holes of stroke play Oct. 9-11 at Lookout Mountain Golf Club. It's the first GSGA championship held at the course. Hayes Brown is the defending champion, and the field is cut to the low 70 and ties after 36 holes.
  Tags: mid-am

- [Society of Seniors Heads to Crown Colony for 7th National Super Senior and 25th John Kline Championships Nov. 1-4](https://www.societyofseniors.com/calendar/7th-national-super-senior-25th-john-kline-championships/) — Society of Seniors
  The 84-player, 54-hole stroke-play event at Crown Colony CC in Lufkin, Texas, is open to players 65 and older with Super Senior (65-69), Legend (70-74) and Super Legend (75+) divisions. Doug Hanzel won the Super Senior title and Jeff Frazier won the Senior title at the Dale Morey Championship in late September.
  Tags: senior
