- [Elite Invitational Girls Play in Honor of Purple Heart Recipients, With Duke Commits Gram and McKoan in the Field](https://theeliteinvitational.org/2026-girls-championship/) — The Elite Invitational
  Every player in the 2026 Girls' Championship, Nov. 5-7 at The King & Bear Golf Club in St. Augustine, Fla., is competing in honor of a Purple Heart recipient. The field includes Duke commits Leah Gram of Jupiter, Fla., and Emma McKoan of Naples, Fla., along with Notre Dame commit Carly Marshall of Temple, Texas, and North Carolina commits Ava Osborne and Macie Rasmussen. Asterisk Talley won last year's girls title at 205.

- [East vs West Rosters Blend Points Rankings and Captain's Picks Ahead of Maridoe Matches Nov. 6-8](https://www.eastwestmatches.com/selection-process-rankings) — East West Matches
  Half of each team qualified through the East-West points rankings, built over two years, and the other half are captain's picks, with the Mississippi River dividing the two sides. Each roster combines senior amateurs 55 and older with a core of mid-amateurs. Players arrive Thursday, Nov. 5, for practice rounds and the draw, followed by four-ball Friday, morning and afternoon foursomes Saturday and singles Sunday at Maridoe Golf Club in Carrollton, Texas.
  Tags: mid-am

  The Golfweek Senior Desert Showdown plays three rounds Nov. 3-5 at Boulder Creek Golf Club in Boulder City, Nev., with Senior, Super Senior, Legend and Super Legend divisions.
