- [AJGA International Pathway Series Heads to Italy for Lombardia Championship at Castello Tolcinasco](https://www.ajga.org/international-pathway-series) — AJGA
  The AJGA's International Pathway Series makes its Italian stop Sept. 30 to Oct. 1 at Castello Tolcinasco Golf Resort and Spa in Pieve Emanuele, run in partnership with the Italian Golf Federation. Players ages 12-19 compete for Performance Based Entry status that opens the door to official AJGA events in the U.S.
  Tags: juniors

- [U.S. Mid-Amateur Quarterfinals Set at The Lido: Hutchins vs. Martinson, Smith vs. Edens, Kittleson vs. Vogt, Bailey vs. Brame](https://championships.usga.org/usmidamateur/2026/scoring.html) — USGA
  Wednesday's quarterfinals tee off at 7:30 AM at The Lido with Sand Valley caddie Matt Hutchins taking on Mikel Martinson, followed by Nate Smith vs. Parker Edens at 7:45 AM, Drew Kittleson vs. Matt Vogt at 8:00 AM, and Andrew Bailey vs. Grady Brame Jr. at 8:15 AM. The semifinals follow in the afternoon, with the 36-hole final set for Thursday.
  Tags: mid-am

- [U.S. Senior Women's Amateur Reaches Quarterfinals and Semifinals Wednesday at Portland Golf Club](https://championships.usga.org/usseniorwomensamateur.html) — USGA
  The 64th U.S. Senior Women's Amateur plays both the quarterfinals and semifinals Wednesday at Portland Golf Club, cutting the bracket from eight to two ahead of Thursday's championship match. The championship runs through Oct. 1.
