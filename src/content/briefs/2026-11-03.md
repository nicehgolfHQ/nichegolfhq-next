- [Rescheduled PING Invitational Will Count Toward the 2026-27 Rolex AJGA Rankings as Seminole Legacy Hosts Nov. 5-8](https://www.ajga.org/tournaments/2026844/information) — AJGA
  The AJGA moved the PING Invitational to Seminole Legacy Golf Club in Tallahassee, Fla., after the October cancellation, with the official practice round Thursday morning. Results count toward the 2026-27 rankings year and won't affect selection of the 2026 Rolex Junior All-America Teams or initial Rolex Tournament of Champions invitations. Preston Hage and Honorine Nobuta Ferry won in 2025.
