- [AJGA Posts Rolex Tournament of Champions Field With Miles Russell, JP Lynch and Charlie Woods Among the Boys](https://www.ajga.org/tournaments/2026147/field) — AJGA
  The AJGA's season-ending invitational plays 72 holes of stroke play with no cut, Nov. 22-25, on the Canyons Course at TPC San Antonio. 2024 champion Miles Russell, Junior PLAYERS winner JP Lynch and Charlie Woods are among the boys listed, and 2023 champion Aphrodite Deng is in the girls field. Players still need to confirm their spots, and the Rolex Junior All-America banquet is Nov. 21.

- [Carlton Woods Invitational Preview: Gwin Defends Against Massa, Deraney and Hunter in The Woodlands](https://www.nichegolfhq.com/midamgolfhq/carlton-woods-invitational/2026-carlton-woods-invitational-field-preview) — nichegolfHQ
  Robert Gwin returns Monday to defend at The Club at Carlton Woods, with 54 holes on the Nicklaus Course Oct. 26-28. Back-to-back Huddleston Cup champion Bobby Massa and three-time Canadian Mid-Am champion Joseph Deraney, the 2018 winner, are in the field along with past champions John Hunter (2022), Colby Harwell (2013, 2016) and Shane Heise (2024). Follow live scoring on the tournament hub.

- [Golfweek Senior Desert Showdown Moves to Boulder Creek for Nov. 3-5](https://golfweekseniornation.com/) — Golfweek Senior Nation
  The event heads to Boulder Creek Golf Club in Boulder City, Nev., after last year's edition at Las Vegas National Golf Club. Jerry Gunthorpe of Ovid, Mich., won the 2025 Senior Division at 1-over 214, three shots clear of Bryan Hoops of Tempe, Ariz. Golfweek's Tournament of Champions follows Dec. 5-7 at PGA National in Palm Beach Gardens, Fla.
