- [Talley Defends ANNIKA Invitational With Four-Shot Win at The Golf Club of Georgia](https://www.ajga.org/tournaments/hilton-grand-vacations-annika-invitational-presented-by-rolex/tournament-history) — AJGA
  Asterisk Talley shot 66-69-67 to finish at 14-under at The Golf Club of Georgia, four ahead of Grace Carter. It's her seventh AJGA win, and she's only the second player in the event's 18 editions to win it back-to-back. The title earns exemptions into the LPGA's Hilton Grand Vacations Tournament of Champions and an Epson Tour event.

  Stephen Behr closed with a 4-under 66 at Lookout Mountain Golf Club and beat Blake Maum and defending champion Hayes Brown on the second playoff hole to win the 45th Georgia Mid-Amateur. Weather cut the championship to 36 holes.
