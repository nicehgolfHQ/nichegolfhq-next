- [Ronin Banerjee Wins by Four and Asterisk Talley Takes the Girls Title at the Rolex Tournament of Champions at TPC San Antonio](https://www.ajga.org/tournaments/2026147/leaderboard) — AJGA
  Banerjee, a 2027 grad from Irvine, Calif., closed with a 6-under 66 to finish at 14-under 274, four clear of Jessy Huebner, who also shot 66, and five ahead of SJ Thomas. Talley, of Chowchilla, Calif., posted rounds of 68-68-69-70 for 13-under 275 and a two-shot win over Mia Clausen. Kelly M. Zhang was third at 9-under 279 after a closing 66.

- [Gasparilla Fills Its Field Through WAGR, Exemptions and a Monday Qualifier Ahead of the 72nd Playing at Palma Ceia](https://gasparillainvitational.com/) — Gasparilla Invitational
  The field for Feb. 18-20 in Tampa, Fla., is built first on the World Amateur Golf Ranking and past Gasparilla results, with exemptions for USGA champions, select Allied Golf Association champions and winners of other national invitationals. The last few spots go to a Monday qualifier during tournament week. It's 54 holes of stroke play with a cut to the low 60 and ties after 36, and Hayes Brown defends.

- [Dixie Senior Amateur Runs Dec. 9-11 at Eagle Trace With Mid-Master, Senior and Super Senior Divisions](https://www.dixieamateur.com/pages/tournament) — Dixie Amateur
  The 54-hole stroke-play event in Coral Springs, Fla., is split into Mid-Master (40-54), Senior (55-64) and Super Senior (65 and older) divisions, with a 9 a.m. shotgun for the Dec. 11 final round. Tee times and pairings are due about a week before the first round. Before that, the Society of Seniors' Ralph Bogart Tournament opens Monday at Abacoa Golf Club in Jupiter, Fla.
