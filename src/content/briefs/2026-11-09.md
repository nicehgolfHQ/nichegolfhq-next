- [Tripp Depoy Wins the PING Invitational by Five at Seminole Legacy](https://www.ajga.org/tournaments/2026844/depoy-claims-the-pinginv-title-with-five-shot-victory) — AJGA
  Depoy of The Woodlands, Texas, a 2027 graduate and Baylor commit, closed with a bogey-free 6-under 66 to finish at 11-under 205 in Tallahassee, Fla., five clear of Aadi Parmar, Tripp Hildreth and Chase Bauer. It was his second AJGA win and first invitational title. The 20th PING Invitational was contested as a boys-only field at Seminole Legacy Golf Club.
