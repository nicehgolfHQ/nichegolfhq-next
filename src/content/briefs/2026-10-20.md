- [Elite Invitational Sets 51-Player Boys and 42-Player Girls Fields for Nov. 5-7 at King & Bear, a Day Before PING's First Round](https://theeliteinvitational.org/) — The Elite Invitational
  The seventh Elite Invitational plays 54 holes with no cut at The King & Bear Golf Club in St. Augustine, Fla., and every player in both fields is competing in honor of a Purple Heart recipient. The rescheduled AJGA PING Invitational, a 72-player field at Seminole Legacy in Tallahassee, starts its first round Nov. 6.

- [Carlton Woods Invitational and Tree Farm Invitational Both Tee Off Oct. 26](https://carltonwoodsinvitational.com/schedule/) — Carlton Woods Invitational
  Robert Gwin is the defending champion at Carlton Woods after winning a 2025 playoff over Joseph Deraney at 4-under 212, and the event plays 54 holes Oct. 26-28 on the renovated Nicklaus Course in The Woodlands, Texas. The inaugural Tree Farm Invitational, pushed from January by weather, plays 36 holes of Stableford Oct. 26-27 at The Tree Farm in Batesburg, S.C.
  Tags: mid-am

- [Reynolds Senior Invitational and Carlton Woods Senior Division Set for Oct. 26-28](https://www.societyofseniors.com/all-events/) — Society of Seniors
  Robert Royak won the Reynolds Senior Division last October at 12-under 204, five shots clear of Michael McCoy, and the event returns to Reynolds Lake Oconee in Georgia next week. The same days, the Carlton Woods Invitational crowns a Senior Division champion (55 and over) on the Nicklaus Course in The Woodlands, Texas.
