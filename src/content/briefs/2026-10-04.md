- [United States Wins World Junior Girls Championship at Brampton as England's Lauren Crump Takes Individual Title](https://www.golfcanada.ca/articles/the-united-states-finish-strong-to-win-the-2026-world-junior-girls-golf-championship/) — Golf Canada
  Team USA closed with an even-par round to finish 15-under and win by five over defending champion Korea, with Team Canada I third at 7-under. England's Lauren Crump shot a final-round 70 to win the individual title at 11-under, one clear of 14-year-old Seoah Kim of Korea, earning an exemption into the 2027 CPKC Women's Open.

- [Durrance and Grant Win Four-Team Playoff for Mid-Am Title at 62nd John R. Williams Four-Ball at Oak Hill](https://www.jrwilliams.org/) — John R. Williams Four-Ball
  Joel Durrance of Orlando and Justin Grant of Ocala, Fla., posted rounds of 65-72-68 for 205 (5-under) and won on the first playoff hole on Oak Hill's East Course. Michael Anderson and Dave Vaclav of Michigan took the Senior division at 200 (13-under), with Vaclav acing the par-3 15th in the final round.

- [Zhong Becomes First Player From China to Win U.S. Senior Women's Amateur at Portland Golf Club](https://championships.usga.org/usseniorwomensamateur/2026/articles/Sherry-Zhong-wins-the-64th-us-senior-womens-amateur.html) — USGA
  Xiaolong "Sherry" Zhong holed a 15-foot birdie putt on the par-3 17th and matched par at the last to edge Amandine Vincent of France in the 18-hole final. Zhong was medalist in her championship debut and tied the lowest round in championship history with a 5-under 67.
