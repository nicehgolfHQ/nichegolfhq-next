- [Ayden Fynaut Defends at the Jones Cup Junior Invitational on Sea Island's Seaside Course Dec. 18-21](https://www.jonescupinvitational.com/junior-invitational/) — Jones Cup Invitational
  Fynaut returns as the defending champion of the boys invitational in Sea Island, Ga., which has been played on the Seaside Course since 2009. A Dec. 6 qualifier on the Retreat Course awards at least four spots. Invitations go to top finishers at the 2026 U.S. Junior Amateur and AJGA invitationals, winners of events such as the Junior Orange Bowl and Western Junior, and top players in the Rolex AJGA Rankings.
  Tags: juniors

- [Fourth Snedeker Memorial Sets March 11-14 at The Lakewood Club, Where Brett Patterson Won the 2026 Title](https://thesnedeker.com/) — Snedeker Memorial
  Registration is open for the 2027 playing in Point Clear, Ala., on Lakewood's Dogwood Course, and players apply for an invitation through the tournament site. Bobby Wyatt and Vince Cave took the 2026 team title. Nate Smith won in 2025 and Jordan Russell won the 2024 edition, then called the Snedeker Memorial Spring Invitational.
  Tags: mid-am

- [Ralph Bogart Divisions Are Set to Play Their Own Tees at Abacoa, From About 6,500 Yards for Seniors to 5,600 for Super Legends](https://www.societyofseniors.com/calendar/43rd-ralph-bogart-tournament/) — Society of Seniors
  The 43rd playing in Jupiter, Fla., starts Monday after a 1 p.m. practice-round shotgun Sunday and the Society of Seniors' annual membership meeting. Super Seniors are slated for about 6,200 yards and Legends for about 5,900. The 54 holes of stroke play finish with Wednesday's 8 a.m. shotgun, and Rick Stimmel won last year at Hammock Beach.
