- [Miles Russell and Asterisk Talley Named 2026 Rolex Junior Players of the Year Ahead of the Tournament of Champions in San Antonio](https://www.ajga.org/news/miles-russell-and-asterisk-talley-named-2026-rolex-junior-players-of-the-year-by-the-ajga) — AJGA
  Russell, 18, of Jacksonville Beach, Fla., joins Phil Mickelson as the only three-time boys winner of the award, adding to his 2023 and 2025 honors. Talley, 17, of Chowchilla, Calif., earns it for the first time after defending her titles at the Fortinet Stanford Invitational and the ANNIKA Invitational. Both will be honored in San Antonio on Nov. 21, the opening day of the Rolex Tournament of Champions at TPC San Antonio.
